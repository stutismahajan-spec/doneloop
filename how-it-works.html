How it works

From a stuck task to a next step you can see.

Complicated tasks become easier when you know what to do next. This is the path, without a promise that someone else will approve, refund, or resolve the matter for you.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your problem.

    Share what is going on, what you have already tried, and how you prefer to be reached. A few concrete sentences are more useful than a perfect summary.

  2. 2

    Organize the relevant information.

    Separate facts, documents, dates, and open questions so nothing important stays buried in a call or a portal screenshot.

  3. 3

    Create a Problem Passport.

    A single structured record holds the summary, the checklist, and the current status. You can see the shape of the task without rereading every message.

  4. 4

    Follow the action plan and track progress.

    Work through practical next steps. Log calls, updates, and deadlines as they happen, including the ones that did not produce an answer.

  5. 5

    Review the outcome and record the final status.

    When the work winds down, the passport keeps what was completed and what remains. Recording a status does not mean a third party has granted the outcome.

What you need to start

  • A description of the task in your own words
  • Anything you have already tried
  • Documents you know about, even if some are missing
  • A way to reach you

What you leave with

A clearer list: what is known, what is missing, and the next action. The sample passport shows that record with fictional data.
