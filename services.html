Services

One service. Six ways the work stays clear.

DoneLoop is a human-assisted way to organize a complicated task. These are not separate products. They are the pieces of a single Problem Passport.

Problem Organization

Organize the customer’s issue and the relevant details so the story has one shape.

  • What the problem is, in plain language
  • Who is involved, and which office or portal owns the request
  • What has already been tried
  • What is still unclear

Problem Passport

A structured record of one problem, so updates do not start from a blank page.

  • A short summary and a reference you can quote
  • The category and the current status
  • The checklist, the plan, and the follow-ups beside it
  • A place to record the final status when you are done on your side

Try the sample passport

Document Checklist

Track known requirements and the items that are still outstanding.

  • Documents you already have
  • Documents someone has asked for
  • A note on where each item came from
  • A clear received or outstanding mark

Action Plan

Organize practical next steps, in an order a person can actually follow.

  • One action at a time, written as something you can do
  • A date to check back, when you know one
  • What does not depend on a third party’s decision
  • What is waiting on someone else

Follow-Up Tracker

Record calls, updates, deadlines, and pending actions so they are not rebuilt from memory.

  • Who was contacted, and how
  • What they said
  • Whether a case number was given
  • When the next check should happen

Progress Record

Review completed actions and the tasks that are still open.

  • What is settled
  • What remains
  • The next step, updated as items close
  • A closure note when you decide the matter is finished on your side
