The problem

A task gets heavy when the path is unclear. You are not short on effort. You are short on a reliable picture of what is required, what you already did, and what should happen next.

Complicated paperwork

Forms, letters, and portals ask for details that are easy to misplace once the thread gets long.

Confusing processes

Requirements change by office, and the instructions rarely say what happens if something is missing.

Missed follow-ups

Calls go unlogged. Deadlines pass because nobody wrote down who said what, or when to check again.

Uncertainty about the next step

You know the outcome you want, but not the single action that actually moves the task forward.

Our solution

DoneLoop organizes the problem into clear steps. You describe it once. The details, the missing pieces, and the next action live in one record instead of five scattered threads.

How it works

Five steps. The full walkthrough is on its own page.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your problem.

    Share what is going on, what you have tried, and how to reach you.

  2. 2

    Organize the relevant information.

    Separate facts, documents, dates, and open questions.

  3. 3

    Create a Problem Passport.

    One record holds the summary, the checklist, and the current status.

  4. 4

    Follow the action plan and track progress.

    Work the next steps and log calls, updates, and deadlines.

  5. 5

    Review the outcome and record the final status.

    Keep what was completed and what, if anything, remains.
